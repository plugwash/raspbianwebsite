# Mirroring Raspbian

**Mirroring the Raspbian repositories:** Use rsync with
*archive.raspbian.org::archive* at a frequency of at least twice a day
and contact me by email at mpthompson AT gmail DOT com to tell me about
your mirror to be included in the list below.
