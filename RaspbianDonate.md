# Donate To Raspbian

## Why donate money?

Raspbian is free of cost to users and doesn't generate any direct income
to support development. Up until now, financial costs related to
development been funded out of pocket by project leader Mike Thompson.
It is hoped that much, if not all, of those costs can eventually be
covered through donations by the community of Raspbian users.

## How is the money used?

Money donated to Raspbian will be used to finance the needs of the
distribution, to pay for hosting, to purchase equipment and to fund the
work of the people behind it.

## How much should you give?

Donations can range in between just a few dollars and several hundred
dollars. The important thing is for users of Raspbian to give something
and to encourage other users of Raspbian to give something.
