# Raspbian eLinux Information

The purpose of the Embedded Linux Wiki (eLinux.org) is to preserve and
present information about the development and use of Linux in embedded
systems as well as open source projects and tools for general embedded
development. eLinux.org is the primary wiki concerning [Raspberry Pi
hardware and software](http://elinux.org/RaspberryPiBoard). Information
concerning Raspbian can also be found on eLinux.org
[here](http://elinux.org/Raspbian).
