# Raspbian Forums

Currently Raspbian doesn't maintain a forum of its own. Instead,
Raspbian is discussed in the forums specific to the hardware currently
supported by Raspbian.

## Raspberry Pi Forums

The Raspbian community forum can be found in the [Raspbian section of
the Raspberry Pi
Forum](http://www.raspberrypi.org/phpBB3/viewforum.php?f=66). Please
visit this forum to ask your questions about how to use Raspbian on your
Raspberry Pi.

## APC Forums

Raspbian for the APC is currently discussed in the [Other Distributions
section of the APC
Forum](http://forum.apc.io/categories/other-distributions). Please visit
this forum to ask your questions about how to use Raspbian on your APC.
