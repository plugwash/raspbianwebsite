# PhotoJim

Hi.

I'm a Canadian Linux/BSD hobbyist whose employment has nothing to do
with the IT industry. I work in the insurance business, and also teach
at a western Canadian business school part-time.

I've been using computers since 1982 and owned one since 1984. I started
with Commodore 64s, worked my way through the Amigas, and eventually
found my way to Windows and open source operating systems.

I tend to like really interesting computer hardware and currently have
running:

486dx2-50 with 32 MB of RAM (running Debian Linux) VAXstation 4000/60
with 104 MB of RAM (running OpenBSD) Sun SPARCstation 10 with dual Ross
90 MHz CPUs and 256 MB RAM (running NetBSD) Sun SPARCstation 20 with
dual Ross 180 MHz CPUs and 512 MB RAM (running NetBSD) Sun Ultra 1 with
167 MHz UltraSPARC CPU and 1 GB RAM (running Gentoo Linux) Alix 2D3 with
500 MHz AMD Geode CPU and 256 MB RAM (running Debian Linux and acting as
my router) Sheevaplug with 512 MB RAM (running Debian Linux and acting
as my offsite server backup computer)

and last, but not least,

Raspberry Pi with 256 MB RAM (running Raspbian Linux)

I also still have a C64, C128, VIC-20 and Amiga 500 kicking around.
