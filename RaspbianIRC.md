# Raspbian IRC

A \#raspbian IRC channel is maintained on the **freenode** discussion
network where the developers of Raspbian hang out and discuss matters
concerning the development and use of Raspbian. Feel free to drop by the
\#raspbian IRC channel to say "hi", ask questions or give feedback
regarding Raspbian.

[Freenode](http://freenode.net/) is the leading provider of on-line
discussion services for Free and Open Source Software communities. If
you don't already have an IRC client, you may use a browser based IRC
client for freenode
[here](http://webchat.freenode.net/?channels=raspbian)
